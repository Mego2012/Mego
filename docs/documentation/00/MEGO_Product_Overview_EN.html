1. What MEGO is

MEGO («MEGO — Nutrition», short name «MEGO» in the web-app manifest) is a mobile-first, installable, offline-capable nutrition tracker delivered as a Progressive Web App (PWA). It covers food logging, calories and macronutrients, body weight and measurements, water, habits and fasting, recipes, meal planning, coaching and weekly/monthly reports. It deliberately does not cover workouts.

The app is bilingual: Arabic with full right-to-left (RTL) layout and English left-to-right, each with light, dark and system themes. It is free to run: hosting, accounts, database and AI scanning all use free tiers without a billing account.

  • Live app: https://mego2012.github.io/Mego/ (GitHub Pages)
  • Source: repository Mego2012/Mego, branch claude/nutrition-tracking-app-989rku

6. Design principles

  • User's numbers first: nutrition is always computed by the app's own engine from the food database; AI estimates are labelled and never replace database values.
  • Nothing silent: goal recommendations are never applied without confirmation; the AI scanner never auto-saves; undo is offered for logging actions.
  • Honest data: a day with no entries is "empty", not zero calories; unknown nutrient values are not treated as 0.
  • Offline first: the local database is the live store; sync runs when online.
  • Privacy by design: administrators cannot read users' records; progress photos never leave the device; photos sent to the AI are downscaled and re-encoded (removing EXIF/GPS) and are not stored.
  • Zero cost: no paid API, database, hosting or subscription.
