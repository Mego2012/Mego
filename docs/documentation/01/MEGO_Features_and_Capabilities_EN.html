Purpose and scope

This document is the complete inventory of what MEGO can do on 10 November 2026: every screen, every major capability, the module that implements it and its verification level. It describes the product as built — not plans. Future work is in MEGO_Product_Roadmap and MEGO_Future_Development_Recommendations. Step-by-step instructions are in the user guides (folder 02).

Note on earlier documents: older repository notes (README.md, docs/FEATURES.md) and the PRD of 7 November describe MEGO as "nutrition only, no workouts". Since then, cardio, workouts and a training coach have been added (verified 10 Nov 2026). This document reflects the current code.

4. AI meal scanner

  • Take a photo or pick one from the gallery (Dashboard, Diary, Add-food sheet). The image is validated and compressed on the phone before sending (src/lib/mealScanner.ts).
  • The photo goes to the private Apps Script backend, which calls the Gemini API free tier. The key is stored only in Apps Script Script Properties. Model chain (free tier only): gemini-3.8-flash → gemini-3.6-flash → gemini-3.5-flash-lite (backend SCRIPT_VERSION 7).
  • The AI identifies items, estimates grams (with a range), raw/cooked state and cooking method, and picks the matching food. Nutrition is always computed by the app's engine from the database. Items not in the database are marked as AI estimates.
  • Nothing is saved until the user confirms on the review sheet. Uncertain items must be ticked as checked.
  • Errors are mapped to precise codes (for example AI_OVERLOADED, AI_TIMEOUT, AI_QUOTA_MINUTE/DAY, AI_KEY_INVALID, AI_REGION). The client retries temporary errors automatically after 3 s and 8 s and keeps the image for a manual retry; one request at a time.
  • Limits: own daily cap AI_DAILY_LIMIT (default 40) and per-user AI_USER_DAILY_LIMIT (default 10) in apps-script/Code.gs; Google's free quota also applies. Free tier can be slow at peak times (live run on 10 Nov: ≈ 64 s, 4 items).

6. Training: cardio, workouts and the training coach

  • Cardio (src/lib/cardio.ts, #/cardio): 10 activity types (walking, treadmill walk, running, cycling, stationary bike, elliptical, stair climber, swimming, rowing, other). Energy is a MET-based estimate (ACSM equations for walking/running with speed and grade), labelled as such. Saved routine default: treadmill 10 min, 3.5 km/h, 13 % incline; default plan 6 sessions × 10 min per week. Week/month charts and history.
  • Workouts (workouts.ts, #/workouts): program generator that keeps the user's split, session logging (weight, reps, RIR, optional RPE, rest timer), strength analytics (Epley e1RM, volume, personal records, trends), double-progression advice.
  • Exercise library and guide: 54 built-in exercises (exercises.ts) in Arabic and English, custom exercises, original pose drawings with start/finish frames and animation (exerciseVisuals.ts), technique text (src/lib/technique/) and a front/back muscle chart (MuscleChart.tsx). No photos or videos.
  • Training coach (trainingCoach.ts): local, rule-based logic on the device — not an AI service. Every answer names its data and confidence; nothing changes without approval. Weekly and monthly reports include a Workouts card.

Training data is stored in the synced kv table (keys cardio:, cardioRoutine, checkin:, program:, activeProgram, workout:, exercise:, recovery:, preferredExercises); the database schema is unchanged (v2).

7. Coach, food intelligence and education

  • Coach tabs Today / Week / Month: messages computed from the user's own logs; "data" messages are separated from labelled "general guidance" with a source (coach.ts).
  • Food rankings (protein, carbs, fats, fibre, low calorie, protein per calorie, nutrient density, cutting, building) using the amino-acid score vs FAO/WHO 2007 and NRF9.3 with goal-dependent weights; fully measured foods are ranked before partial-data foods (foodIntel.ts, intelCatalog.ts with 83 foods). Comparison table.
  • Education: 13 articles with cited sources (education.ts).
  • Insights (insights.ts): only from saved data, need ≥ 3 fully logged days, weight trends from 7-day averages.

8. Recipes and meal planning

  • 101 built-in recipes (recipeLibraryData.ts + recipeLibraryData2.ts) in Arabic and English. Nutrition is computed from the food database at runtime (recipeEngine.ts, recipeRefMap.ts); missing ingredients are shown as missing and never invented (example: labneh).
  • Filters, ratings, favourites; add to diary or plan; save as editable recipe; suggestions that fit what is left today; "What can I cook today?" from selected ingredients.
  • Planner: week view, reusable day plans, automatic week plan, copy last week, shopping list with check-off, planned vs actual.

9. Body, habits and reports

  • Progress: weight, waist and optional measurements (one record per date), 7-day average chart, weekly/monthly averages, rate of change, estimated target date, pace warning; progress photos stored on the device only.
  • Water & habits: water target and quick add, custom habits with streaks, fasting timer with history, reminders (fire only while the app is open or in the background — reminders.ts).
  • Reports: weekly and monthly with day ledger, averages over fully logged days, consistency, weight, macro split, water, fibre, top foods, previous-period comparison, Workouts card; export CSV, Excel (XLSX) or print-to-PDF (reports.ts, exporters.ts).

11. Cross-cutting qualities

  • Bilingual: full Arabic RTL and English LTR (src/lib/i18n*.ts); themes light, dark, system.
  • Offline-first: IndexedDB (Dexie) plus a Workbox service worker that precaches the app shell and the USDA database.
  • Zero cost: GitHub Pages, Firebase Spark, Apps Script, Gemini free tier — no billing account.
  • Honesty rules: empty days are never zero; estimates are labelled; recommendations are never auto-applied.
