Purpose and scope

This PRD records the product goals, target users and the functional and non-functional requirements of MEGO, and traces every requirement to its implementation in the repository with a status: Implemented, Partially (implemented but limited or not fully verified) or Not implemented. It describes the product as built on 7 November 2026; it is not a wish list. Proposed future work is in MEGO_Product_Roadmap.

1. Product goals

IDGoalMeasure of success
G1Make accurate daily nutrition tracking fast on a phone, in Arabic and English.A food can be found and logged in a few taps; values come from a verified per-100 g database.
G2Give users goals and targets they understand and control.Targets derived from a transparent formula and never applied without confirmation.
G3Turn logs into honest feedback.Reports, insights and coaching use only saved data; empty days are not counted as zero.
G4Protect personal data.Each account's data is readable only by its owner, enforced server-side.
G5Run at zero cost.No billing account on any service.
G6Work offline.Logging works without a connection; data syncs later.

2. Users and personas

PersonaDescriptionKey needs
P1 – Goal-driven trackerArabic-speaking adult (18+) losing fat while keeping muscle, logs every meal on an Android phone.Fast Arabic search, cooked/raw distinction, protein focus, weekly progress.
P2 – Casual guestWants to try the app without creating an account.Full functionality on one device, no sign-up.
P3 – Multi-device userUses phone and laptop.Account, live sync, password reset, data export.
P4 – Project ownerMaintains the personal food sheet and the services.Google Sheet Hub sync, control of the AI key, backups, admin rights.
P5 – AdministratorModerates the public service.User counts, suspend/reactivate, review submitted foods — without seeing private data.

3. Scope and non-goals

  • In scope: nutrition logging, targets, body measurements, water/habits/fasting, recipes, planning, coaching, reports, accounts, sync, backups, AI meal photo analysis.
  • Non-goals (by design): workout tracking; medical diagnosis or treatment; paid services; administrator access to users' private records; creating administrators from inside the app.

6. Constraints and assumptions

  • Free-tier limits apply (Firebase Spark quotas, Apps Script quotas, Gemini free quota); when reached, the service stops for the day.
  • Gemini free-tier terms: accounts from EU/EEA/UK/Switzerland are refused by the AI proxy (AI_RESTRICTED_COUNTRIES in src/lib/firebase.ts).
  • Users must be 18 or older (registration checkbox and age ≥ 18 on step 2).
  • The app is a web app; there are no store (Play Store / App Store) packages.
