IMPORTANT — this roadmap is a PROPOSAL. Every item below is a suggestion derived from open items recorded in the repository (docs/*.md) and in the documentation brief. None of it is a commitment. No dates are promised; items are grouped by priority (Now / Next / Later), and the owner decides what is done and when.

Purpose and scope

This document collects the open items that remain after the 10 November 2026 state of MEGO and arranges them into a proposed order. For each item it names the source in the repository, the dependency and who can act on it. Technical improvement ideas with more detail are in MEGO_Future_Development_Recommendations.

1. Starting point (what is already live)

  • Public app on GitHub Pages; Firebase (Spark) accounts and Firestore in me-central2; Google Sheets Hub with Apps Script backend; Gemini free-tier meal scanner (backend SCRIPT_VERSION 7).
  • Release 1.3-training: cardio, smart energy adjustment, goals advisor, workouts and the rule-based training coach, exercise guide (54 exercises), data check, recipe nutrition linked to the food database.
  • Tests: unit 195, browser e2e 57/57, touch/scroll 103/103, storage-loss scenario, live verification, live exercise pages 54/54.

6. Explicitly out of scope today

  • Paid services, billing accounts or paid AI models (zero-cost principle).
  • Administrator access to users' private records.
  • Creating administrators from inside the app.
  • Medical diagnosis or treatment.
  • Google Sheets as the multi-user database (evaluated and rejected in docs/ARCHITECTURE.md).

Store packages (Google Play / App Store) do not exist today; no decision on them is recorded — Not verified as a goal.
