Purpose and scope

This guide explains daily food logging in the Diary (#/diary): adding, editing, copying and deleting entries, day status and day type, water, per-day targets, and the AI meal scanner (photo → recognised foods → your database values → review → log), including what happens when the AI service is slow or busy and how automatic and manual retries work. Searching the food database and barcode scanning are covered in detail in MEGO_Food_Search_and_Database_Guide.

1. The Diary screen

Open the Diary from the bottom navigation. From top to bottom:

  • Date navigation and a week strip: seven days (starting on your configured week start) with a small ring for how much of the training-day calorie target was eaten; the ring turns to the warning colour above 105 %. Tap a day to open it.
  • Day panel: a calorie ring (eaten / target), "Remaining" or "Over" in kcal, the day status chip, the day-type selector, protein/carbs/fat bars, optional nutrients, and the water line.
  • Scan strip: buttons Take a photo and Upload from gallery for the AI meal scanner.
  • Quick actions: Copy yesterday, Copy day to…, Mark day complete / Mark day partial / Auto status, Targets for this day.
  • Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, Snacks — each with its calories, entries and an Add button.

2. Adding food

  1. Tap Add on a meal (or "Add to Lunch" in an empty meal). The sheet "Add food — <meal>" opens; you can switch meal at the top.
  2. Choose a source from the tabs: All (search), Recent (last 30 used), Frequent (30 most used), Favorites, History (previous diary entries), Saved meals, Recipes. A raw / cooked / ready filter and a barcode button sit next to the search box.
  3. Pick a food, enter the quantity and unit (grams or the units the food supports, e.g. piece, slice, tbsp, serving) and tap Log food.
  4. For a recipe, enter the number of Servings; the kcal total is previewed before you log.
  5. Choosing a saved meal logs all its items at once ("Logged n item(s)"); items whose food or recipe no longer exists are skipped and counted.

Every add shows a toast with Undo. Accidental double taps are blocked: the same food, quantity and unit cannot be added twice to the same meal within 1.5 seconds.

Values are frozen per entry: "Values are saved with this entry at logging time and do not change if the food is edited later." Raw and cooked foods are separate records; the app never converts between them automatically.

2.1 Making logging faster: completing your food database

The Foods page offers two one-time helpers that only add information (both save a local backup "Before import" first and never change an existing value):

CardWhat it does
Complete my food database → "Check what is missing" → "Add to my foods"Adds every ingredient used by the recipe library as a food with USDA FoodData Central values, and every library recipe whose ingredients are all known as a ready meal (category "وجبات (مكتبة الوصفات)") with a portion "حصة (وصفة)" — 100 ready meals with the current library (all 101 recipes except the one that needs labneh). Log these by the serving portion: values per 100 g refer to the raw ingredient weight, so logging by serving is exact. Ingredients without a verified reference (for example labneh) are never estimated; they are listed as missing, and recipes that need them are skipped until you add that food yourself from its label. Foods that already exist are not duplicated.
Arabic & English names → "Check what is missing" → "Add the missing names"Fills only empty Arabic or English names from the hand-translated list public/fooddb/food-names.json. Existing names are never changed; foods still missing a name are listed so you can add it in the food editor.

The added foods and names sync to a signed-in account like any other food. Details: MEGO_Food_Search_and_Database_Guide.

3. Editing, copying and deleting entries

Tap an entry to open its sheet:

ActionResult
Change quantity, unit, meal or date → SaveValues are recalculated from the stored basis; a preview of kcal and macros is shown first. Toast with Undo.
DuplicateAdds a copy on the same date
Copy to date…Choose date and meal
DeleteAfter confirmation; Undo restores it

Meal and day copying: under each meal use Copy meal to… or Save as saved meal; in the quick actions use Copy yesterday or Copy day to…. If the destination day already has entries, MEGO asks: "{date} already has n item(s). Copy anyway? (they will be added, not replaced)". Every copy can be undone.

4. Day status, day type and per-day targets

  • Status: No entries / Partially logged / Fully logged. An empty day shows "Nothing logged — not counted as a zero-calorie day". Use Mark day complete or Mark day partial to set it manually, or Auto status to return to the automatic rule (past days reaching the configured % of the calorie target count as complete; see MEGO_Reports_Guide).
  • Day type: the selector shows the default from your training weekdays; you can force Training day or Rest day for this date only.
  • Targets for this day: enter kcal, protein, carbs and fat for this date only, or tap Use default to remove the override.

5. Water

The water line shows ml drunk / water target (default 3,000 ml). Tap +250 or +500. More water and habit tools are on the Water & habits page.

6. AI meal scanner — taking and analysing a photo

The scanner recognises the foods in a photo, estimates grams, raw/cooked state and cooking method, and matches each item to your food database. All nutrition numbers are then calculated by the same engine as manual logging. Nothing is saved until you press Confirm.

6.1 Requirements

ModeBackend usedLimits
Signed-in accountThe public AI backend named in the app's cloud configuration; your sign-in token is sent with the requestPer user per day (default 10, Script Property AI_USER_DAILY_LIMIT) and a global daily limit (default 40)
Guest / owner deviceYour private Apps Script backend connected in Settings → MEGO Nutrition Hub — sync & backupDaily limit AI_DAILY_LIMIT (default 40)

The Gemini key is stored only in the backend's Script Properties; it never reaches the phone. Only free-tier Flash models are used (gemini-3.8-flash → gemini-3.6-flash → gemini-3.5-flash-lite); nothing is charged.

6.2 Steps

  1. In the Diary (scan strip, or inside "Add food") tap Take a photo (opens the camera) or Upload from gallery.
  2. Optional: write a Note for the AI (max. 300 characters), e.g. "restaurant meal, cooked with oil, 2 eggs".
  3. Tap Analyse photo. Status: "Preparing photo…", then "Analysing your meal… (5–30 s)". You can Cancel at any time. With the free tier, analysis can take longer at peak times (a live test on 10 Nov 2026 took ≈ 64 s).

6.3 What is sent (privacy)

  • The photo is re-encoded as JPEG on the phone at max. 1,024 px (768 or 640 px if still too large). Re-encoding removes all EXIF metadata (GPS location, device, time).
  • Accepted input: image files up to 30 MB (JPEG, PNG, WebP, GIF, AVIF; HEIC/HEIF only where the browser can decode it).
  • Together with the photo, only your food names, state and category are sent (as short keys f1, f2…) — no personal data. Avoid people or documents in the photo.

7. AI meal scanner — reviewing and saving

The review sheet shows the Detected meal name, the reminder "Weights from a photo are estimates. Correct them if you weighed the food.", and one line per item:

ElementMeaning
✓ From your databaseItem matched to one of your foods; nutrition comes from that food
⚠ AI estimate — not in your databaseNo match; the AI's per-100 g estimate can be used only if you approve adding it as a new food
Confidence High / Medium / Low (%)Below 60 % the item must be checked
Grams, cooking method, stateEditable; "AI estimate lo–hi g — please correct if you know the weight" shows the range
Warningsraw/cooked mismatch (match rejected), hidden fat or sauce, unknown database row
Choose from database / Change food, "Did you mean:"Pick the right food yourself; suggestions are never applied automatically
Add to my database as a new foodCreates a new food marked "AI photo estimate — unverified, please review" in category "تقدير من صورة"
I checked this itemRequired for low-confidence items, unmatched items and hidden fat/sauce

You can remove items (✕), Add ingredient from the database, choose Add to meal and date (the meal is pre-selected from the time of day: 04–11 breakfast, 11–16 lunch, 18–23 dinner, otherwise snacks), and see the Meal total (with "Includes AI-estimated values (marked ⚠)" when relevant).

Confirm & add to diary stays disabled until: at least one item exists, every weight is 1–3,000 g, every unmatched item is either matched, approved as new or removed, and every uncertain item is ticked. Saving is all-or-nothing; the same food detected twice is merged into one entry. The toast "Meal added: n item(s)" offers Undo, which deletes the entries and archives foods created by this scan.

8. Errors, automatic retry and manual retry

8.1 Behaviour

Phone (one request at a time)            Backend (Apps Script, SCRIPT_VERSION 7)
  analyse ──────────────────────────────► model 1 ─ 503 overloaded ─► next free model at once
     │                                       │  500 / 504 ─► retry after 2 s, then 4 s ─► next model
     │ temporary error?                      ▼
     ├─ yes ─► wait 3 s, retry ─► wait 8 s, retry (max 2 automatic retries)
     └─ no  ─► show the error; photo is kept
  • Temporary errors (retried automatically, max. 2 times, after 3 s and 8 s): AI overloaded, server error, slow response (timeout), connection problem, AI busy. While retrying the sheet shows "Retrying automatically (n/2) — reason…".
  • A per-minute quota error is retried automatically only if Google asks to wait 20 seconds or less.
  • Never retried automatically: daily quota, invalid key, configuration, region, model unavailable, bad or unsupported image, per-user limit.
  • No retry is attempted while the phone is offline ("You are offline … your photo is kept").
  • The phone waits up to about 280 seconds for an answer, because the backend itself may try several models.
  • The same image is never sent twice at the same time.

8.2 Manual retry

After an error the sheet shows the message, a short "Service response" detail when Google returned one, and the button Try again. For temporary errors it adds: "Your photo is kept — tap Retry, no need to choose it again." The prepared (compressed) image is reused; it is not re-encoded. Change photo lets you pick another one.
