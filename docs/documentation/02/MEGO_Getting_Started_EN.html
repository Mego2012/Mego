Purpose and scope

A short, practical start for first-time users: open MEGO, install it on the phone, decide between a guest session and an account, set your first daily targets and log your first food. It takes about ten minutes.

1. Open MEGO and install it as an app

MEGO is a web app (a "PWA") — there is no app-store download. It runs in the browser and can be added to the home screen so it opens full-screen like a normal app.

  1. On an Android phone open Chrome and go to https://mego2012.github.io/Mego/.
  2. Tap the Chrome menu ⋮ → Add to Home screen (Chrome may call it Install app) → confirm.
  3. Open MEGO from the new icon. It opens full-screen with the MEGO splash screen and no browser bar.

Why install? Installed apps are more likely to receive persistent storage from the browser, which protects data saved on the phone. Settings → Data & backups shows "Persistent storage granted" or "Persistent storage not granted by the browser (install the app to improve this)."

MEGO also works offline: everything you log is saved on the phone and synchronised when the connection returns.

2. Guest or account?

On first opening, the welcome screen offers three buttons: Create account, Sign in, and Continue without an account (this device only).

You can start as a guest and create an account later: Settings → Account → Sign in / Create account. During sign-in or registration MEGO offers to copy the guest diary into the new account (see MEGO_Registration_and_Login_Guide).

3. Set your first targets

3.1 With an account

Registration step 2 ("About you") asks for sex, age, height, current weight, target weight, activity level and goal. Step 3 ("Your daily targets") shows calculated calories, protein, carbs and fat. You can edit each number before pressing Create my account.

3.2 As a guest

  1. Open More → Settings and scroll to Profile.
  2. Choose your Goal, fill in sex, age, height, current/start/target weight and Activity level.
  3. Under "Recommended for this goal" check the suggested values. Press Save, then Use these targets and confirm — this replaces both the training-day and rest-day targets.
  4. Optional: in Nutrition targets set different training-day and rest-day values and tick your training weekdays. Press Save.

How the numbers are calculated is explained in MEGO_Profile_and_Goals_Guide. They are a starting point only — adjust them according to your weekly average weight.

4. Log your first food

  1. On the Dashboard tap Add food (or open Diary and tap Add next to a meal).
  2. Choose the meal at the top (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, Snacks).
  3. Type at least two letters, e.g. "egg" or «بيض». Results come from your own foods and from the built-in food database (USDA).
  4. Tap a food. Check the raw/cooked chip — "Make sure you weighed it in the same state as this record."
  5. Enter the Quantity and choose the Unit (for example 2 piece, or 120 g). The preview shows calories and macros.
  6. Tap Log food. A message "Added: …" appears with Undo.

The Dashboard dial now shows calories remaining. Add water with the +250 ml quick button.

5. What to do next

  • Log all meals of the day; past days count as "Fully logged" automatically when they reach 60% of the calorie target (adjustable), or mark them yourself (MEGO_Food_Tracking_Guide).
  • Weigh in a few times a week from Progress → Add measurement.
  • Try Suggestions on the Dashboard or the Meal Planner (MEGO_Meal_Planning_Guide).
  • Guests: download a backup file regularly (Settings → Data & backups → Download backup file).
