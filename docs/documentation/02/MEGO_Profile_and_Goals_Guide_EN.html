Purpose and scope

This guide explains how to enter your personal profile, how MEGO turns it into a recommended calorie and macronutrient target, how to set training-day and rest-day targets, and how to use the Goals page (#/goals): weekly progress bars, the Goals advisor (recommendations), the weekly check-in, fitness and cardio goals, and the smart cardio–calorie adjustment shown on the Dashboard. It does not cover account registration (see MEGO_Registration_and_Login_Guide) or the scientific background in detail (see MEGO_Daily_Calorie_Target_Calculation).

Important: every number MEGO calculates is a starting point, not a medical prescription. Targets are never changed automatically — you always confirm a change first.

2. Entering your profile (Settings → Profile)

  1. Open Settings and tap the Profile chip.
  2. Enter Your name (max. 30 characters, optional).
  3. Choose your Goal from the list (table below). If you choose "Custom goal", type a free-text description.
  4. Fill in sex, age, height (cm), current weight, starting weight, target weight (kg), starting waist (cm, optional), weekly weight-change target (kg, e.g. −0.25), activity level and training days per week.
  5. Tap Save. The profile is saved; your targets are not changed by this.

3. The recommendation and how it is calculated

Below the profile fields, the box "Recommended for this goal" recalculates live while you type:

BMR  (Mifflin-St Jeor) = 10 × weight + 6.25 × height − 5 × age + 5 (male) / − 161 (female)
TDEE = BMR × activity factor
kcal = max(BMR, round(TDEE × (1 + adjustment) / 10) × 10)     ← never below BMR
protein = weight × protein-per-kg
fat     = max(0.8 g × weight, 25 % of kcal ÷ 9)
carbs   = (kcal − 4 × protein − 9 × fat) ÷ 4
expected weekly change ≈ (kcal − TDEE) × 7 ÷ 7700 kg

Worked example (the default starting profile: male, 26 years, 170 cm, 72 kg, Active, goal "Fat loss while preserving muscle"):

StepResult
BMR = 720 + 1,062.5 − 130 + 51,658 kcal
TDEE = 1,658 × 1.7252,860 kcal
kcal = round(2,860 × 0.82 / 10) × 102,350 kcal
Protein = 72 × 2.2158 g
Fat = max(57.6, 2,350 × 0.25 / 9 = 65.3)65 g

If your saved targets differ from the recommendation, MEGO says "Your current targets are custom … they are kept unless you choose to replace them" and shows the button Use these targets. Tapping it asks for confirmation ("Replace your current training and rest-day targets … Past days are not changed.") and then writes the recommendation into both the training-day and rest-day targets.

4. Nutrition targets: training day, rest day, tolerances

Settings → Nutrition targets holds the targets that the Diary, Dashboard and reports use:

  • Training-day targets and Rest-day targets: kcal, protein, carbs, fat. Under each set the app shows the check "4P + 4C + 9F = … kcal" so you can see whether the macros add up to the calories.
  • Training weekdays: tap the weekday chips (default Saturday–Thursday training, Friday rest). A date's day type comes from this list unless you override it for one day in the Diary.
  • Consistency tolerances: calorie range ± % (default 10) and protein allowance − % (default 5).
  • Week starts on (Saturday, Sunday or Monday; default Saturday).
  • Count past days with entries as fully logged (default on) and the threshold "A past day counts as fully logged at ≥ % of calorie target" (default 60).
  • Water target in ml (default 3,000).

Press Save at the bottom of the card. Changing targets never rewrites past diary entries. A single day can have its own targets via Diary → "Targets for this day" (see MEGO_Food_Tracking_Guide).

6. The Goals advisor (recommendations)

The advisor (src/lib/goalAdvisor.ts) reviews trends, not single readings: the 7-day weight average and a 28-day regression line, waist change over 14 days, your adherence in the last 14 days, cardio in the last 7 days, and your latest check-in (if it is from the last 14 days). It produces suggestions only.

6.2 Safety rule for fat-loss goals

For Fat loss, Fat loss while preserving muscle and Body recomposition, a suggested calorie increase may reduce the deficit but never creates a surplus: the training-day target may not exceed 90 % of your estimated TDEE. If there is less than 50 kcal room, the suggestion is replaced by "A calorie increase would bring you close to maintenance … focus on recovery and protein instead". Otherwise the increase is cut to the available room.

6.3 Applying a suggestion

  1. Tap Apply next to a suggestion that has an action.
  2. A confirmation shows the current and the new targets ("Current → new").
  3. Confirm. The change is applied to both training-day and rest-day targets. Calorie changes are absorbed by carbohydrates (Δkcal ÷ 4 g); protein and fat stay the same. A protein change keeps calories and moves the same grams out of carbohydrates.

Nothing is applied automatically. The advisor is local rule-based logic in the app, not an AI service.

The weekly cardio goal is sessions × minutes (default 6 × 10 = 60 min). Until you save one of these fields, the card says "Default: one 10-min cardio session after each of your 6 training days (not saved yet …)". After saving it shows "Saved by you: … sessions × … min". MEGO does not read a step counter, so daily step goals are not tracked.

Cardio and calorie target (mode):

  • Smart (only justified extra) — default; see section 8.
  • Off (never adjust) — the daily target always stays as set.

Add-back share (%) (Smart mode only): "Automatic by goal" (default) or a fixed 0, 25, 50, 75 or 100 %. Automatic means 50 % for fat-loss, recomposition and custom goals, 75 % for maintenance and healthy lifestyle, 100 % for muscle gain and performance.

Your saved cardio routine (default: treadmill walk, 10 min, 3.5 km/h, 13 % incline, moderate) is managed on the Cardio page; see MEGO_Progress_and_Insights_Guide for cardio statistics.

8. Smart cardio adjustment of the daily target

The Dashboard card Today's calories with cardio shows: Base target → Cardio (estimate) → Extra allowed → Adjusted target → Eaten → Remaining. Tap Why? to see the reasons in plain language. The adjustment is shown on the Dashboard only; your saved targets are never changed by it.

The logic (src/lib/energy.ts):

  1. Planned cardio is already in your target. For any activity level above Sedentary, your planned weekly cardio minutes count as already included in TDEE. Minutes from the 6 previous days use up the plan first; only today's minutes beyond the plan are "extra".
  2. Only net energy counts. Net kcal = (MET − 1) × kg × hours, because the body would have burned about 1 MET at rest anyway.
  3. Extra resistance workouts beyond your planned resistance days in the last 7 days count as net energy of a moderate session (3.5 MET).
  4. Partial add-back. Allowed extra = extra net kcal × add-back share, rounded to 10 kcal, capped at 400 kcal per day.
  5. Fat-loss safeguard. For loss/recomposition goals the adjusted target stays ≤ 0.9 × (TDEE + extra net energy), so exercise can never move the day to maintenance or surplus.

Example: base target 2,000 kcal, goal fat loss (share 50 %), plan 60 min/week already completed on previous days, today an extra 30-min session with ≈ 150 kcal net → allowed = round(150 × 0.5 / 10) × 10 = 80 kcal → adjusted target 2,080 kcal. Logging only your planned routine never raises the target.

All cardio calories are estimates (typically ± 20–30 %) and are labelled as such.
