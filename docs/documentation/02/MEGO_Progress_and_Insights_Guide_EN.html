Purpose and scope

This guide explains how MEGO shows your progress: weight and body measurements on the Progress page (#/progress), the rate and target-date estimate, progress photos, the automatically generated smart insights, cardio statistics on the Cardio page (#/cardio) and strength progress on Workouts → Progress (#/workouts?tab=progress). Weekly and monthly reports are described in MEGO_Reports_Guide; targets and the Goals advisor in MEGO_Profile_and_Goals_Guide.

A general rule throughout MEGO: trends are judged from averages, never from a single reading. Daily weight fluctuates with water, salt and digestion.

1. Recording weight and body measurements

  1. Open Progress and tap Add measurement (the round + button).
  2. Choose the date (today or earlier; future dates are not allowed).
  3. Fill in any of the fields — at least one is required:
FieldUnitAccepted values
Weightkgmore than 20 and less than 400
Waist, Chest, Hips, Arm, Thigh, Neckcmmore than 5 and less than 300
Body fat %%more than 1 and less than 70
Notestextfree text

Tap Save. The message "Saved (one record per date — saving again updates it)" explains the rule: there is only one measurement record per date; saving the same date again merges the new values into it. Tap a row in Weight history to edit or delete it (deletion asks "Delete the measurement of <date>?").

When the saved weigh-in is the most recent one, your profile's current weight is updated automatically.

3. Rate of change and estimated target date

MEGO uses an honest projection (progressEstimate in src/lib/reports.ts):

  • It needs at least 6 weigh-ins spread over at least 14 days within the last 28 days. Otherwise it shows "Need ≥ 6 weigh-ins over ≥ 2 weeks for an estimate."
  • Rate = slope of a least-squares line through those weigh-ins × 7 (kg/week); also shown as % of the current 7-day average.
  • Target date = today + ⌈remaining kg ÷ rate⌉ weeks, shown only when the pace is healthy or fast, more than 0.2 kg remain, and the result is at most 156 weeks (3 years).
PaceRuleMessage
Stable|rate| < 0.1 kg/weekWeight is stable
Awaymoving opposite to the target (more than 0.3 kg remaining)Trend is moving away from the target weight
Healthy≤ 1 % of body weight per weekHealthy pace
Fast> 1 % of body weight per weekFaster than 1 % … risk of muscle loss; consider eating a bit more

Example: 8 weigh-ins over 21 days whose regression slope is −0.05 kg/day → rate −0.35 kg/week; at a 7-day average of 80 kg that is −0.44 %/week (healthy). With 4 kg to go: ⌈4 / 0.35⌉ = 12 weeks → target date ≈ today + 84 days.

4. Progress photos

  • Tap + Add photo (opens the camera or file picker). The photo is compressed on the phone and stored with today's date.
  • Photos are shown in a grid with their dates; × deletes a photo after confirmation.
  • Photos are kept in the device database only. They are not part of the sync tables, so they are not uploaded to the cloud or the Hub and are not included in backup files. Keep your own copy if they matter to you.

5. Smart insights

The Dashboard and the weekly report show Smart insights generated strictly from saved data (src/lib/insights.ts). Each insight states the numbers it is based on. Window: the last 7 days compared with the 7 days before.

TopicRule

Not enough dataFewer than 3 fully logged days → no nutrition conclusions
ProteinAverage below target × (1 − protein tolerance) → low (with the gap in g); otherwise OK
CaloriesAverage above / below target ± calorie tolerance → high / low; otherwise OK
FatFat above target + 10 % on ≥ 60 % of complete days → high; within ± 10 % → OK
CarbohydratesWithin ± 10 % → OK; otherwise above/below (informational)
ConsistencyCalorie consistency up or down by ≥ 10 points vs the previous 7 days
Weight trendAverage of the last 7 days vs the 7 before (≥ 2 weigh-ins in each); change < 0.2 kg = stable
PlateauLoss goal, two stable weeks in a row, consistency ≥ 70 % with ≥ 5 complete days → plateau suggestion
Loss too fastWeekly drop larger than twice your weekly goal and more than 1 kg
Gain while cuttingLoss goal, weight up, good adherence
FluctuationLatest weigh-in differs from the 7-day average by ≥ 0.8 kg → explained as normal fluctuation

Insights never change your targets. Calorie-target suggestions come only from the Goals advisor and require your approval.

6. Cardio progress

On the Cardio page you log sessions (activity, duration, date/time, intensity, and depending on the activity distance, speed or incline; optional calories you measured yourself) or tap Log my routine (default routine: treadmill walk 10 min, 3.5 km/h, 13 % incline, moderate). The page shows:

  • Last 7 days (minutes) and Last 30 days (minutes) charts.
  • Trends: minutes of the last 7 days vs the previous 7 days, Average session length (30 days) and the Most frequent activity (30 days).
  • A WHO marker: "WHO recommends at least 150 min of moderate activity per week" (informational only).
  • Cardio history with each session's estimated calories, the method used (ACSM walking/running equation, Compendium value for speed or intensity, or "Entered by you") and the body weight used (latest weigh-in on or before that date, otherwise the profile weight).

Calories are estimates: gross kcal = MET × kg × hours; net kcal = (MET − 1) × kg × hours. They can differ from your real expenditure by 20–30 %. The weekly cardio goal and its progress appear on the Goals page and in the reports (minutes vs goal, change vs the previous period).

7. Strength and workout progress

Open Workouts → Progress:

  • Strength progression: one row per exercise you have logged, with "Last w kg × r · best e1RM e kg" and a trend chip: Improving / Stable / Declining with % change, or "Need 4+ sessions".
  • Trend rule: average estimated 1RM of the last 3 sessions vs the 3 before; above +2 % improving, below −2 % declining.
  • Estimated 1RM uses the Epley formula: weight × (1 + reps ÷ 30), for sets of ≤ 12 reps. It is a comparison tool between sessions, not a max to test.
  • Tap an exercise for its detail sheet: personal records (best estimated 1RM, heaviest weight, most reps in a set, best session volume in kg), an e1RM chart of the last 20 sessions, the next-step recommendation with reasons and confidence, and the session table (sets as weight×reps@RIR, e1RM, volume).
  • A weekly sets per muscle chart compared with your goal (default 14 sets per muscle) and the planned sets of the active program, plus a recovery card.

The weekly and monthly reports include a Workouts card: workouts done vs planned, consistency %, hard sets, volume (kg), new PRs and strength trends (↑ improving · ↓ declining), each with the change vs the previous period. The training coach is local rule-based logic, not an AI service (see docs/TRAINING-METHODOLOGY.md).
