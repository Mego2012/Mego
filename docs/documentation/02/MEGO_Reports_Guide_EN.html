Purpose and scope

This guide explains the Weekly Report (#/reports/week) and Monthly Report (#/reports/month): how days are classified, how averages and consistency are calculated, how the comparison with the previous period works, and how to export a report as CSV, Excel (XLSX) or PDF.

1. Opening a report and navigating periods

  1. Open the menu and choose Weekly Report or Monthly Report.
  2. The title shows the period (e.g. "1 Nov – 7 Nov 2026" or "November 2026").
  3. Use the arrows (Previous / Next) to move by one week or one month.

The week starts on the day set in Settings → "Week starts on" (default: Saturday).

2. Day status and the day ledger

Every day has one of three statuses. A day without entries is never counted as a zero-calorie day.

StatusRule
No entriesnothing logged on that date
Fully loggedyou marked the day "Mark day complete" in the Diary, or (automatic) it is a past day whose calories reach at least 60% of the calorie target (setting "A past day counts as fully logged at ≥ % of calorie target", default 60) while "Count past days with entries as fully logged" is on (default on)
Partially loggedhas entries but is not complete (today stays partial until you mark it complete)

A manual choice in the Diary ("Mark day complete" / "Mark day partial") always wins; "Auto status" returns to the automatic rule.

At the top of the report three chips count Fully logged days, Partially logged days and Days with no entries. Below is a calendar-style ledger: one cell per day, coloured by status, with a marker for "Days over calorie target". In the monthly ledger each date sits under its weekday.

3. Averages (fully logged days only)

The report states: "Averages use fully logged days only. Partial and empty days are excluded (never counted as zero)."

Average calories = sum of calories on fully logged days ÷ number of fully logged days
(same for protein, carbs, fat; targets are averaged over the same days)

Example: a week with 4 fully logged days (2,000 / 2,200 / 1,900 / 2,100 kcal), 2 partial days and 1 empty day → average = 8,200 ÷ 4 = 2,050 kcal. The partial and empty days do not lower the average.

Four gauges show Average calories, Average protein, Average carbs and Average fat as "average / target", each with a "vs previous" difference.

4. Consistency and goal completion

All counts below use fully logged days. Tolerances come from Settings → "Consistency tolerances" (defaults: Calorie range ± 10 %, Protein allowance − 5 %).

IndicatorDefinition
Days within calorie target|calories − target| ≤ target × 10 %, shown as "days / fully logged days"
Days hitting protein targetprotein ≥ target × (1 − 5 %)
Calorie consistencydays within calorie target ÷ fully logged days × 100
Protein consistencydays hitting protein ÷ fully logged days × 100
Goal completiondays with both calories in range and protein hit ÷ fully logged days × 100
Days over calorie targetcalories > target × (1 + 10 %)

If there are no fully logged days, these values show "—".

5. Weight, water, fiber and top foods

  • Weight card: Starting weight, Ending weight, Average weight, Weight change vs previous (difference of average weights), Waist change, number of weigh-ins, and a weight chart with the 7-day average line.
  • Macro split (% of calories): share of calories from protein (×4), carbs (×4) and fat (×9) based on the averages.
  • Average water over days on which water was logged ("days / days in period").
  • Fiber: shown only if every entry on the fully logged days has fiber data; below 25 g/day shows "below the 25–38 g recommended". Otherwise: "Not enough fiber data…".
  • Top foods (by calories): up to 8 foods with count and calories.

6. Charts, comparison and day table

  • Daily calories and Daily macronutrients charts.
  • Comparison — Current / Previous: table with Average calories, Average protein, Average carbs, Average fat, Calorie consistency, Protein consistency, Average weight and Fully logged days for the current and previous period (previous week / previous month) and the difference.
  • Diary table: each day with status, calories, protein, carbs, fat (— for empty days; ▲ marks over target) and the target (kcal/protein).

7. Monthly extras and insights

  • Monthly report only: Best consistency days (fully logged days ranked by a score = share of the four goals — calories, protein, carbs, fat — within tolerance) and, when there are more than 3 such days, Lowest consistency days (3 days).
  • The last card shows Smart insights (weekly) or Overall progress summary (monthly), generated from the last 7 days of saved data up to the end of the period. See MEGO_Progress_and_Insights_Guide.

8. Exporting: CSV, Excel, PDF

ButtonFile / resultContent
Export CSVnutrition-week-<first date>.csv or nutrition-month-…csvone row per day (date, status, day type, kcal, protein, carbs, fat, target kcal, target protein, calories in range, protein hit — 1/0 for fully logged days), then a summary block (day counts, averages, consistency, goal completion, weight start/end/average, waist change)
Export Excelnutrition-week-….xlsxsheet "Summary" (as CSV) and sheet "Diary" (every entry of the period: date, meal, food, state, quantity, unit, grams, kcal, protein, carbs, fat)
Print / save PDFbrowser print dialogthe report page without navigation buttons; choose "Save as PDF" as printer

Column labels follow the current app language. Exports run entirely on your device.
