Purpose and scope

This manual describes every screen of the MEGO nutrition app (web address https://mego2012.github.io/Mego/) as it works in the released version. It explains what each screen shows and what each button does, using the exact button labels of the English interface. Detailed step-by-step guides for individual tasks are listed under "Related documents". Arabic is the default interface language; you can switch to English in Settings → Language · Theme.

1. Navigation and common elements

On a phone, a bottom bar shows four screens plus More. On a wide screen (tablet/desktop) all screens are listed in a side menu.

Bottom barUnder "More"
Dashboard · Diary · My Recipes · ProgressCoach · Foods · Meal Planner · Water & habits · Weekly Report · Monthly Report · Settings

The header shows the screen name and a sync chip with the current state: "Sync not configured", "Synced", "Syncing…", "Sync error" or "Offline — changes are saved on this device". When the phone is offline a banner says: "You are offline. Everything you log is saved and will sync when you reconnect."

Most actions show a short message at the bottom of the screen (a "toast"). After adding, copying or deleting food the toast offers Undo.

2. Dashboard (home)

  • Greeting and date with two chips: the day status (Fully logged / Partially logged / No entries) and the day type (Training day / Rest day).
  • Calorie dial: calories remaining (or "Over") and the percentage of the target. Below it: Goal − Food = Remaining.
  • Macro bars for protein, carbs and fat, plus fiber/sugar/sodium when the foods contain them.
  • Suggestions button showing "Remaining now: … kcal · P … g · C … g · F … g"; it opens recipe suggestions that fit what is left.
  • Quick actions: Add food, +250 ml (water), Coach, Weigh-in.
  • Scan a meal (AI): Take a photo / Upload from gallery (see section 4.3).
  • Coach card with one message for today (or the tip of the day).
  • Meal tiles (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, Snacks) with calories; tap one to open it in the Diary. Below: "Meals logged" (out of 4) and "Daily goal completion".
  • Water card (amount / target, +500 button), Goal progress (current vs target weight, weekly weight trend, estimated weeks to target), This week summary with a 7-day calorie chart, and Smart insights (based only on your saved data from the last 7 days).

3. Diary

  • Date navigation and a week strip: each day has a small ring showing how much of the calorie target was eaten (it turns to the warning colour above 105%).
  • Day panel: calorie ring (eaten / target), remaining or over, status chip, and a Day type selector (default, Training day, Rest day). Macro bars and a water line with +250 / +500.
  • Scan buttons for the AI meal scanner.
  • Tool row: Copy yesterday · Copy day to… · Mark day complete · Mark day partial · Auto status · Targets for this day.
  • Meal sections (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, Snacks), each with an Add button. Under a meal with entries: Copy meal to… and Save as saved meal.
  • Tap an entry to edit it: Quantity, Unit, Meal, Date, then Save; or Duplicate, Copy to date…, Delete. Note shown: "Values are saved with this entry at logging time and do not change if the food is edited later."

A day with no entries is shown as "Nothing logged — not counted as a zero-calorie day". Full details: MEGO_Food_Tracking_Guide.

4. Adding food

4.1 The "Add food" sheet

The sheet has a meal selector, scan buttons, a search box, a barcode button and a raw/cooked filter (All, Raw, Cooked, Ready / processed). Tabs: All, Recent, Frequent, Favorites, History, Saved meals, Recipes. Typing two or more letters searches your own foods and the built-in Food database (USDA, about 12,900 foods, Arabic and English search). If nothing fits you can Create "…" as my food or Search packaged products online (Open Food Facts).

4.2 Quantity

After choosing a food, enter a Quantity and a Unit (g, kg, ml, L, cup, tbsp, tsp; piece/slice when the food has a piece weight; serving when it has custom servings). Quick buttons (50–250 g, or 0.5–4 units) and a live preview of calories and macros are shown. Press Log food.

4.3 AI meal scanner and barcode

Take a photo or Upload from gallery → optional note → Analyse photo → review every detected item (weight, food match, "I checked this item") → choose meal and date → Confirm & add to diary. Nothing is saved before you confirm. The barcode button looks a product up in your own foods first, then in Open Food Facts; you check the values and press Save food & log. Details in MEGO_Food_Tracking_Guide.

5. Foods (food database)

Your personal food list. You can search, filter (Min protein, Max kcal, Max carbs, Max fat, raw/cooked), add a New food, edit a food (names in Arabic and English, raw/cooked state, preparation method, values per 100 g / per 100 ml / per serving from the label, piece/slice weight, density, custom serving sizes, optional fiber/sugar/sodium), view its Change history, and Delete (archive) it — past diary entries keep their values and the food can be restored via Show deleted. Import / export offers Import CSV / Excel, Export CSV, Export Excel, Export my foods (JSON) and Import my foods (JSON). Data issues flags foods with missing values, calories that do not match the macros, duplicate names and similar. Signed-in users can Suggest for the shared database; an administrator reviews the suggestion. See MEGO_Food_Search_and_Database_Guide and MEGO_Recipes_and_Custom_Foods_Guide.

6. My Recipes

Five tabs:

  • Recipe library — 101 ready recipes with filters (High protein, Low calorie, Low carb, Chicken, Eggs, Rice, Fish & seafood, Meat, Sandwiches & wraps, Quick (≤ 20 min), Meal prep, High fiber, Vegetarian, Breakfast, Favorites). A recipe page shows ingredients, steps, tips, prep/cook time, difficulty and nutrition per serving calculated from the food database. Buttons: Add to diary, Add to plan, Save to my recipes (editable), Favorite and Your rating.
  • Suggestions — recipes that fit the calories and macros remaining today, with reasons such as "covers protein" or "fits calories".
  • What can I cook? — tick the ingredients you have (oil and butter are assumed); matching recipes are listed with what is missing.
  • My recipes — your own recipes: New recipe, ingredients, servings, totals per serving, Duplicate, Add to diary.
  • Saved meals — groups of foods saved from the Diary or created with New saved meal; press Log to add them.

7. Meal Planner

Week view with previous/next arrows. Buttons: Suggest a week plan, Shopping list, Copy last week's plan. Each day shows Planned vs Actual calories and buttons Edit, Copy plan to…, Log planned meals and Clear. The card Reusable day plans holds named plans you can apply to several days. Full guide: MEGO_Meal_Planning_Guide.

8. Water & habits

  • Water: buttons +150, +250, +330, +500, +750 ml and −250 to correct; progress toward the daily target (default 3,000 ml, editable in Settings) and the last 14 days.
  • Daily habits: tick habits each day (three examples are pre-filled), Add habit, delete; a streak counter and the day completion are shown.
  • Fasting timer: set the Fasting goal (hours, default 16), Start fast / End fast; the running time and "Goal reached" are shown, plus a Fasting history.
  • Reminders: water, meal and weigh-in reminders with a time; Allow notifications. Note from the app: reminders fire while the app is open or in the background; Android may delay them when the app is fully closed.

9. Progress

Add measurement saves weight, waist, chest, hips, arm, thigh, neck and body fat % for a date (one record per date — saving again updates it). The screen shows the weight chart (daily weigh-ins and a 7-day average line), weekly and monthly averages, change from start, waist trend, rate of change per week with a pace message, and an estimated target date (needs at least 6 weigh-ins over at least 2 weeks). Progress photos stay on the phone only. See MEGO_Progress_and_Insights_Guide.

10. Weekly and Monthly Reports

Use Previous / Next to move between weeks or months. The report shows average calories and macros, days within the calorie target, days hitting protein, consistency percentages, weight start/end/average and change, waist change, daily calorie and macro charts, macro split, top foods, average water, a fiber indicator, best and lowest consistency days and a comparison with the previous period. Averages use fully logged days only; partial and empty days are excluded (never counted as zero). Export: Export CSV, Export Excel, Print / save PDF. See MEGO_Reports_Guide.

11. Coach

  • Today — "Your day so far", "Complete my macros" with exact gram options, and the tip of the day.
  • Week / Month — reviews based on fully logged days (at least 3 per week, at least 8 per month). Suggestions only: "MEGO never changes your targets automatically."
  • Rankings — food rankings by Protein, Carbohydrates, Healthy fats, Fibre, Low calorie, High protein low calorie, Nutrient dense, For cutting, For muscle building; rank for Fat loss / Maintenance / Muscle gain; Compare up to 3 foods; tap a food for the score breakdown and amino-acid chart.
  • Learn — short articles with sources (educational information, not medical advice).

13. Account screens

When the app is opened without being signed in, a welcome screen offers Create account, Sign in and Continue without an account (this device only). Registration has 3 steps (account, about you, daily targets). A suspended account sees "Account suspended" with only a Sign out button. Privacy policy and Terms of use pages are drafts. Full guide: MEGO_Registration_and_Login_Guide.
