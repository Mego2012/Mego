Purpose and scope

MEGO classifies foods along several independent axes: a free-text category, a weighing state (raw / cooked / other), a photo group used for pictures and placeholders, and — for the 83 foods of the Food Intelligence catalog — nutrient groups, origin, a whole-food flag and diet/allergen tags. This document describes each axis exactly as implemented in src/lib/types.ts, refFoods.ts, sheetImport.ts, foodPhotos.ts and intelCatalog.ts. None of these classifications changes nutrition values.

1. Categories in the data

1.1 User foods

Food.category is a free-text string. It is not an enumeration; its value depends on the source:

SourceCategory value
Owner Hub (sheet)Column category of Food_Database, as typed by the owner (Arabic). The full list of Hub category values is not stored in the repository.
USDA reference foodThe USDA category name (see 1.2), copied when the food is materialized
AI photo estimate added as food«تقدير من صورة» ("estimate from photo")
Custom / Open Food FactsWhatever the user enters (may be empty)

The category is used for: filtering reference search results (RefFilter.category), choosing a placeholder photo group (section 3), and in the energy check explainEnergy, where a category matching /(خضار|فاكهة|veg|fruit)/i marks a food as vegetable/fruit.

1.2 USDA reference database categories

public/fooddb/usda.json stores categories once in meta.categories (195 entries); each row stores the index. There are 24 SR Legacy food groups and 171 FNDDS "WWEIA" categories (the two sets do not overlap). The 24 SR Legacy groups:

Baked Products · Snacks · Sweets · Vegetables and Vegetable Products ·
American Indian/Alaska Native Foods · Restaurant Foods · Beverages · Fats and Oils ·
Sausages and Luncheon Meats · Dairy and Egg Products · Poultry Products · Pork Products ·
Breakfast Cereals · Legumes and Legume Products · Finfish and Shellfish Products ·
Fruits and Fruit Juices · Cereal Grains and Pasta · Nut and Seed Products · Beef Products ·
Meals, Entrees, and Side Dishes · Fast Foods · Spices and Herbs ·
Soups, Sauces, and Gravies · Lamb, Veal, and Game Products

"Quality Control Materials" and "Baby Foods" are excluded by the build script. FNDDS examples: "Milk, whole", "Yogurt, Greek", "Plant-based milk", "Dips, gravies, other sauces", "Baby food: mixtures". Largest categories in the shipped file: Beef Products 954, Vegetables and Vegetable Products 814, Baked Products 517, Lamb, Veal, and Game Products 464, Poultry Products 383, Beverages 366.

Ranking use: reference search gives +1.5 to rows whose category starts with a basic group (Poultry|Beef|Pork|Lamb|Finfish|Dairy and Egg|Fruits|Vegetables|Cereal Grains|Legume|Nut and Seed|Fats and Oils|Breakfast Cereals|Spices|Beverages|Sweets|Baked Products|Snacks) and treats "Fast Foods", "Restaurant Foods" or "Branded" categories as branded (−4 unless the query names a brand).

2. Food state: raw, cooked, other

Type FoodState = 'raw' | 'cooked' | 'other': raw = weighed raw/dry; cooked = weighed after cooking; other = prepared/processed/powder/not applicable. The engine never converts between raw and cooked weights (toGrams); duplicates are judged by name and state, so "rice, raw" and "rice, cooked" can coexist.

2.1 Inference for USDA foods (inferState, refFoods.ts)

  1. If the name contains the word raw, dry, unprepared or uncooked → raw.
  2. Else if it contains cooked, roasted, boiled, grilled, broiled, fried, baked, braised, stewed, steamed, poached, microwaved, scrambled, toasted, sauteed, simmered, pan-browned, pan-fried, smoked or canned → cooked.
  3. Otherwise → other.

Result over the shipped 12,879 rows: other 7,253 · cooked 3,590 · raw 2,036. The materialized food gets stateLabel نيء / مطبوخ / محضّر.

2.2 Inference for sheet/imported foods (normalizeState, sheetImport.ts)

The Hub's state column is used when it is exactly raw/cooked/other; otherwise the label is normalized in this order:

OrderLabel containsState
0empty, "—" or "-"other
1نيء, نيئ, ني (at end), خام, raw, جاف, dry, uncookedraw
2مصنّع, مصنع, محضّر, محضر, مسحوق, processed, prepared, powderother
3مطبوخ, مطهي, مقلي, مشوي, مسلوق, cooked, boiled, grilled, fried, bakedcooked

Because "processed" is tested before "cooked", a label like «مطبوخ/مصنّع» becomes other. The original text is kept in stateLabel.

3. Photo groups

PhotoGroup = 'protein' | 'dairy' | 'legume' | 'grain' | 'fruit' | 'veg' | 'fat' | 'nut' | 'sweet' | 'drink' | 'other' (src/lib/foodPhotos.ts). A food gets a photo only when the match is unambiguous (74 Wikimedia Commons photos, credits in public/food-img/CREDITS.md):

  1. By FDC id (from sourceRef "USDA FDC 171477 …" or an id like S171477) against each key's list.
  2. By English name regex, unless the name looks like a dish/mixture/processed product (e.g. with, sauce, soup, salad, fried, juice, powder, baby food…) or a non-listed oil.
  3. By Arabic whole-word regex on the normalized name, unless excluded (e.g. مع, صلصه, شوربه, عصير, مقلي…).

The first matching key wins (specific keys before generic ones). Without a photo, a designed placeholder is shown, its group guessed from category + name by groupFromText, checked in this order:

4. Food Intelligence catalog classification

INTEL_CATALOG (src/lib/intelCatalog.ts) lists 83 foods; each points to exactly one USDA record (values are read from the shipped database, never typed). Forms are chosen as usually eaten: meats, fish, grains and legumes cooked; fruit, vegetables, nuts and dairy as sold.

AttributeValuesUse
groups'protein' | 'carb' | 'fat' | 'fiber' (a food may be in several, e.g. lentils = protein, carb, fiber)Which ranking lists the food appears in
origin'animal' | 'plant'Shown only, never used to rank
wholetrue = minimally processed whole food (grains: whole grain); false e.g. white rice, pasta, oils, butter, canned tuna, protein powdersDisplayed / used by the ranking methodology (see MEGO_Food_Rankings_and_Coach_Methodology)

5. Diet and allergen tags

DIET_TAGS = ['meat', 'poultry', 'fish', 'shellfish', 'dairy', 'egg', 'nuts', 'peanut', 'soy', 'gluten', 'sesame']. Tags are assigned per catalog id (foods without tags have none):

TagCatalog foods
poultrychicken breast, turkey breast
fishtuna canned, tuna cooked, cod, tilapia, haddock, salmon, mackerel, sardines
shellfishshrimp
meatground beef 95%, top sirloin, lamb leg
egghard-boiled egg, egg white
dairyGreek yogurt nonfat and lowfat, cottage cheese 1%, feta, skim milk, 1% milk, whey protein, butter
soytofu, tempeh, edamame, soy protein powder
glutenwhole-wheat bread, whole-wheat pita, bulgur, pasta, whole-wheat pasta, couscous, barley, oats (oats: "often cross-contaminated with gluten unless certified")
nutsalmonds, walnuts, pistachios, cashews
peanutpeanut butter
sesametahini

Exclusion logic (excludedTags(diet, exclude)): start with the user's excluded tags (Settings.foodExclusions); vegetarian adds meat, poultry, fish, shellfish; vegan additionally adds dairy and egg. Foods carrying an excluded tag are left out of Coach / Food Intelligence suggestions. These preferences are never used by the nutrition engine. Tags exist only for the 83 catalog foods; other foods are not tagged.

6. Verification status

  • Verified in repository: all rules, lists and counts above (from code and the shipped usda.json).
  • Not verified: the actual category values in the owner's live Hub (not stored in the repository). Allergen tags are a convenience filter, not a medical allergen guarantee; completeness of tagging for real products is not verified.
