Purpose and scope

This document describes how the Food Intelligence rankings (Coach page → rankings) score and order foods, and how the rule-based Nutrition Coach produces its daily, weekly and monthly messages and food suggestions. Sources: src/lib/foodIntel.ts, src/lib/intelCatalog.ts, src/lib/coach.ts, src/pages/Coach.tsx, src/pages/Dashboard.tsx. The coach is local logic computed from the user's own data — it is not an AI service and never changes targets or diary entries. All numbers below were computed by running this code on 10 Nov 2026 with the shipped USDA files.

1. Data and catalog

  • Catalog: 83 foods (intelCatalog.ts), each pointing to exactly one USDA FoodData Central record, in the form usually eaten (meat, fish, grains, legumes cooked; fruit, vegetables, nuts, dairy as sold). Groups: protein, carb, fat, fiber; a food may be in several.
  • Values: kcal and macros, fibre, sugar, saturated fat, sodium from usda.json; MUFA, PUFA, ALA, EPA+DHA+DPA, minerals, vitamins and 11 essential amino acids from usda-extra.json. A catalog id missing from USDA is skipped.
  • Origin (animal/plant) and whole food flags are part of the catalog; origin is displayed but never used in scoring.
  • Diet filters: the ranking can be limited to foods allowed by the user's diet (any / vegetarian / vegan) and exclusions (meat, poultry, fish, shellfish, dairy, egg, nuts, peanut, soy, gluten, sesame).

5. Ordering and missing data

coverage = Σ weights with data ÷ Σ all weights
order: (1) coverage = 1 first, (2) higher score, (3) lower kcal

A food with an unmeasured indicator is never given an estimated value: its score uses the remaining weights, but it is placed after every fully measured food, in a "partial data" group. In the protein ranking 4 foods are in that group (e.g. whey powder and shrimp have no amino-acid data); 2 in cutting and 4 in building.

6. Worked example: protein ranking for a cut

Chicken breast, roasted (FDC 171477): 165 kcal, protein 31.02 g, saturated fat 1.01 g, fibre 0 g per 100 g.

proteinDensity = min(1, 31.02/165×100 ÷ 20) = min(1, 18.80 ÷ 20) = 0.940
aminoScore     = 1 (limiting leucine ratio 1.27, capped at 1)
proteinAmount  = min(1, 31.02 ÷ 30)        = 1
lowSatFat      = 1 − (1.01/165×100 ÷ 3)    = 1 − 0.204 = 0.796
fiberDensity   = 0
score = 0.40×0.940 + 0.25×1 + 0.10×1 + 0.15×0.796 + 0.10×0 = 0.845 → 84.5   (rank 7 of 39)

Lentils, cooked (FDC 172421; 116 kcal, P 9.02, fibre 7.9): density 0.389, amino 0.983, amount 0.301, lowSatFat 0.986, fibre 1 → 0.1555 + 0.2457 + 0.0301 + 0.1478 + 0.1 = 0.679 → 67.9 (rank 14). With the build weights the order shifts toward protein amount and amino score.

7. Amino-acid score and NRF9.3

Amino-acid score (FAO/WHO/UNU 2007 adult pattern, mg per g protein: His 15, Ile 30, Leu 59, Lys 45, Met+Cys 22, Phe+Tyr 38, Thr 23, Trp 6, Val 39). For each essential amino acid: (g per 100 g × 1000 ÷ protein g) ÷ reference; the lowest ratio is the limiting amino acid; score = min(1, lowest ratio). Not corrected for digestibility (PDCAAS/DIAAS need data USDA does not publish) — the app states this.

Lentils: Met 0.077 + Cys 0.118 = 0.195 g/100 g → 0.195×1000 ÷ 9.02 = 21.6 mg/g ÷ 22 = 0.983 → score 0.98, limiting Met+Cys
Almonds: lysine ratio 0.597 → score 0.60, limiting lysine

NRF9.3 (Fulgoni, Keast & Drewnowski 2009), per 100 kcal: Σ of %DV (each capped at 100) for protein, fibre, vitamins A, C, E, calcium, iron, magnesium, potassium minus Σ %DV of saturated fat, total sugar (used instead of added sugar, which USDA SR does not report) and sodium. Daily Values (FDA 2016): protein 50 g, fibre 28 g, vit A 900 µg, vit C 90 mg, vit E 15 mg, Ca 1300 mg, Fe 18 mg, Mg 420 mg, K 4700 mg, sat. fat 20 g, sugar 50 g, sodium 2300 mg. If any of the 12 nutrients is not reported, NRF9.3 is not computed.

8. Pros, cons and uses

Generated from the values (prosCons): pros — protein ≥ 20 g; protein ≥ 15 g per 100 kcal; amino score ≥ 1; fibre ≥ 5 g; EPA+DHA ≥ 0.5 g; ALA ≥ 2 g; unsaturated ≥ 70 % of fat (fat ≥ 5 g); kcal ≤ 60; whole-grain carb. Cons — limiting amino acid; saturated fat ≥ 5 g; kcal ≥ 400; sodium ≥ 400 mg; refined carb; carb food with fibre < 1 g and carbs > 10 g. Uses — cutting, building, fuel, satiety, omega-3, small portions.

9. Nutrition Coach — daily

Computed from today's diary, targets, goal and diet (dailyCoach); Home shows the first message or, if none, the tip of the day.

ConditionMessage
no entries todayno-log message with today's kcal and protein target; nothing else is invented
remaining protein > 10 gprotein short (split over meals if > 45 g) + up to 5 food options
protein ≥ 95 % of targetprotein done
fat ≥ 90 % of target and protein gap > 15 gfat near limit + lean options (fat ≤ 3 g/100 g)
over target by > 50 kcalover calories
fibre known for every entryfibre below 25 g (WHO 2023) message with source
fibre unknown for some entriessays how many entries have fibre data

Protein options (proteinOptions): per meal gap = min(remaining protein, 45 g); foods taken in protein-ranking order; grams = gap ÷ protein per 100 g × 100, rounded to 5 g (min 5 g); portions > 450 g or above 105 % of remaining kcal are skipped.

Remaining: protein 60 g, kcal 600, goal cut → gap = min(60, 45) = 45 g
Tuna, yellowfin cooked (29.15 g P, 130 kcal /100 g): 45 ÷ 29.15 × 100 = 154.4 → 155 g → 202 kcal, 45.2 g P
next: cod 195 g (205 kcal, 44.5 g), turkey breast 150 g (204 kcal, 44.3 g), haddock 225 g, canned tuna 230 g

Complete my macros (completeMacros, needs ≥ 80 kcal remaining): combinations of the top-8 protein foods (P ≥ 9 g, fat < 12 g), top-8 carb foods (carbs ≥ 15 g, P < 10 g) and optionally one of the top-6 fat foods (fat ≥ 40 g). Grams are solved carbs → protein → fat, rounded to 5 g; combos over 110 % of remaining kcal or with unrealistic grams are dropped; ranked by relative deviation from remaining P, C, F; one combo per protein food. Example for remaining 700 kcal / P 50 / C 70 / F 20 (cut): tilapia 185 g + pear 460 g + canola oil 15 g → 632 kcal, P 50.1, C 70.1, F 20.5.

10. Nutrition Coach — weekly, monthly and tips

  • Weekly (needs ≥ 3 fully logged days): calorie consistency (good if ≥ 70 %), protein days hit (warning if < half), average fibre vs 25 g, repetitive protein source (one food > 45 % of protein), diversity (distinct foods; good if ≥ 15), over-target days (warning if ≥ 3), improvements vs previous week (consistency +10 points, more protein days).
  • Monthly (needs ≥ 8 fully logged days): average kcal vs target (good within 5 %); weight change start→end (≥ 2 weigh-ins; < −0.3 kg down, > +0.3 kg up, else stable) compared with the goal direction; general guidance to reduce/increase intake labelled as guidance with source; weekly rate from the progress estimate (warning when the pace is fast).
  • Tip of the day: one of 10 general tips, chosen by the date number modulo 10 (e.g. 2026-11-10 → 20261110 mod 10 = 0 → "protein spread").
