Purpose and scope

This document lists the personal data MEGO handles, where each category is stored, who can access it, and the technical measures that protect it — exactly as implemented in the code. It supports the privacy policy draft (MEGO_Privacy_Policy_Draft, folder 12), which is the single user-facing policy. Legal classifications (for example whether body measurements count as health data under applicable law) are open items for a qualified lawyer and are not decided here (see docs/LEGAL-REVIEW.md).

4. Protection measures

4.1 In transit

All network traffic uses HTTPS (Firebase, Apps Script, Gemini via Apps Script, Open Food Facts, GitHub Pages, Google Fonts).

4.2 At rest in the cloud

Firestore and Firebase Authentication are Google-managed services; encryption at rest is provided by Google (Google's general statement; not separately verified for this project). Access is enforced by firebase/firestore.rules: owner-only, active accounts only, validated record shape, size limit 300,000 characters per record.

4.3 On the device

  • Separate IndexedDB database per account; switching account reloads the app.
  • The app requests persistent browser storage (navigator.storage.persist()) to reduce eviction; Settings shows whether it was granted.
  • Data is not encrypted by the app and stays on the device after sign-out (see section 7).

4.4 Data minimisation

  • The profile document accepts only seven fields (displayName, email, status, country, ageConfirmed, createdAt, lastActiveAt); the e-mail must equal the e-mail of the signed-in token.
  • Error reports: kind (≤ 40 chars) and message (≤ 400 chars written, rule limit 500) without diary content.
  • Exports and backups never contain the Hub token.

4.5 Age

Registration requires an 18+ confirmation (checkbox, AGE_REQUIRED), stored as ageConfirmed: true, and step 2 requires age ≥ 18. Age is self-declared; it is not verified.

5. AI meal scanner and third parties

  • Before upload the photo is downscaled and re-encoded as JPEG through a canvas, which drops all EXIF metadata (GPS location, device, time) (src/lib/mealScanner.ts).
  • The request carries the photo, a list of food names from the user's database and an optional note (≤ 300 characters). It goes to the owner's Apps Script, which calls the Gemini API free tier. MEGO stores nothing about the meal; only a daily counter per uid is kept.
  • Privacy-policy text in the app (pp_4) states that on the free tier Google may use the content to improve its products and human reviewers may see it, and asks users not to photograph people or documents.
  • Results are never saved automatically; the user reviews them first.
  • Accounts registered in the EU/EEA, UK or Switzerland are refused the scanner (country is self-declared at registration).

7. Gaps and recommendations

  • Local data remains on the device after sign-out and is not encrypted; on shared devices, recommend "Delete account" or clearing site data. A "remove this device's copy on sign-out" option does not exist.
  • Account deletion does not remove the user's foodSubmissions documents, and error reports cannot be linked to a user (no uid) and are not removed.
  • The privacy policy and terms are drafts; operator identity, legal basis, retention period for inactive accounts and cross-border transfer wording are open (docs/LEGAL-REVIEW.md).
  • Rotate the Gemini key and backend token that were shown in a chat (MEGO_Security_Audit_and_Known_Risks).
