Purpose and scope

This document lists the automated tests that prove MEGO's nutrition numbers are calculated correctly: food logging per gram and per unit, raw versus cooked records, recipes, remaining macros, targets and reports, USDA reference values, the energy (Atwater) check, cardio energy estimates, the smart calorie adjustment, goal recommendations and strength metrics. For each case it gives the input, the expected value asserted by the test, how the value is derived, and the test that checks it. All values below are copied from the test files; the unit suite passed 199/199 on 10 November 2026.

1. Calculation basis

  • Every food stores values per 100 g. An entry's macros = per-100 g value × grams ÷ 100 (macrosFor in src/lib/nutrition.ts).
  • Raw and cooked versions are separate records; the app never converts between them.
  • Each diary entry keeps a snapshot of its values, so later edits to a food do not change the past.
  • Displayed values are rounded by roundMacros (kcal to whole numbers, macros to 0.1 g); values are stored with 0.01 precision so that sums of many small entries do not drift; rounding to whole kcal happens only when displaying.

9. What the tests guarantee — and what they do not

  • Guaranteed by tests: the arithmetic of the app (conversions, sums, rounding, targets, reports, estimates) gives the exact values above, and missing data is never invented.
  • Not guaranteed: that a food's per-100 g values are correct for the product actually eaten — the app uses the values in the user's table, USDA or Open Food Facts as they are. Cardio calories are labelled as estimates. Gemini photo analysis gives estimates that the user must review; matched foods use database values.
  • The app is not a medical device; see MEGO_Nutrition_Disclaimer and MEGO_Medical_Disclaimer.
