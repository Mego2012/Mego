Purpose and scope

This draft sets out what users may and may not do when using MEGO, and how the rules are enforced. It expands the in-app Terms text tos_3 ("Do not abuse the service, attempt to access other accounts or overload the AI scanner. Accounts may be suspended for abuse.") and forms part of MEGO_Terms_of_Service_Draft (section 5). It applies to signed-in users and, where relevant, to guest use. Every statement about enforcement describes what the code does as of 10 November 2026. Text in [square brackets] must be completed by the owner and legal counsel. This draft is not legal advice.

1. Principles

MEGO is a free personal nutrition tool running on free-tier services with limited quotas (Firebase Spark plan, Apps Script quotas, Gemini API free tier). Use it for your own nutrition and fitness tracking, in a way that does not harm other users, the operator or the providers.

2. Your account

  • One account belongs to one adult person (18+). Do not share your account or use another person's account.
  • Keep your password private (tos_2). Passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication.
  • Provide a truthful age confirmation and country. The country determines whether the AI scanner is available (not offered in the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland); do not give a false country to bypass this.

3. Prohibited activities

You must not:

  1. attempt to access, read or change other users' data or accounts, or to obtain administrator rights;
  2. probe, scan or test the security of the service, its backend or its Firebase project, except as agreed in writing with the operator [responsible-disclosure terms — to be decided];
  3. overload the service: automated or bulk requests, scripts that create accounts, mass uploads or repeated requests designed to exhaust free quotas;
  4. circumvent limits such as the daily scan limits, the region restriction or the account suspension;
  5. upload unlawful, offensive or third-party confidential content in any field, food name, recipe, note or photo;
  6. submit deliberately false or misleading nutrition values to the shared food database;
  7. use the service to harm yourself or others, or present its estimates as professional medical or dietary advice to others;
  8. reverse-engineer the backend to extract secrets (keys, tokens) or reuse them outside MEGO;
  9. [any further prohibited uses — to be decided after legal review].

4. AI photo scanner

  • Photograph meals only. Do not photograph people, documents or anything confidential: on the Gemini free tier Google may use the content to improve its products and human reviewers may see it (pp_4).
  • Do not overload the scanner (tos_3). Each signed-in user has a daily scan limit (default 10 per day) and the backend has a global daily limit (default 40); the operator can configure both. Failed analyses are not counted.
  • AI results are estimates; nothing is saved until you review and confirm (tos_8).

5. Shared food database submissions

Signed-in users can suggest a personal food for the shared database ("Suggest for the shared database"). Submit only values you have checked against the product label or a reliable source. Submissions stay pending until an administrator approves or rejects them; approved foods become visible to every user. A pending submission can be withdrawn by its author. No self-promotion to administratorThe admins collection cannot be written by any client; a regular account cannot list profiles or suspend others (refused live, HTTP 403)

Record size and typeEach synced record is limited to fewer than 300,000 characters and to the app's known tables; older copies cannot overwrite newer ones
Error reportsOnly signed-in users; fixed fields; message under 500 characters; readable only by administrators
AI scanner abuseBackend verifies the Firebase sign-in, requires an active profile, refuses restricted countries and applies per-user and global daily limits

7. Suspension and its effects

7.1 Administrators may suspend accounts that break these rules (tos_3, tos_9). A suspended account cannot read or write its cloud records or use the AI scanner. Suspension does not delete data ("Your data is kept and nothing has been deleted"). An administrator can reactivate the account.

7.2 In the current version the suspended screen offers only sign-out; export during suspension is not available in the app, although in-app text tos_9 says "you can still export your data". [The owner must decide how a suspended user obtains a copy of their data and correct the wording.]
