Purpose and scope

This draft states that MEGO is not a medical service and describes the health-related limits of its features: nutrition targets, weight tracking, fasting timer, workouts, exercise technique pages, recovery indicators and the coach. It expands the in-app texts pp_8 ("MEGO provides general nutrition information and is not a medical device or a substitute for professional advice."), tos_5, pp_7, pp_9, learn_disclaimer, exd_disclaimer and rl_disclaimer. Every factual statement describes what the app does in the repository as of 10 November 2026. Text in [square brackets] must be completed by the owner and legal counsel. This draft is not legal advice.

1. Not a medical device or medical advice

1.1 MEGO provides general nutrition and fitness information. It is not a medical device, does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease, and is not a substitute for advice from a doctor, registered dietitian or other qualified health professional.

1.2 Nothing in the app — targets, reports, rankings, coach messages, Goals-advisor suggestions, educational articles or exercise pages — is a personal medical recommendation. Educational articles state: "Educational information, not medical advice. For medical conditions, consult a qualified professional."

1.3 No health professional reviews your data or the app's messages. The coaches in MEGO are local rule-based logic; the training coach "needs no AI service".

2. Who should consult a professional first

Consult a qualified professional before making significant dietary changes, especially with a medical condition, pregnancy or an eating disorder (tos_5). In particular (non-exhaustive):

  • diabetes, kidney, liver, heart or thyroid disease, or any condition treated with medication;
  • pregnancy or breastfeeding;
  • a current or past eating disorder — calorie counting and weighing may be harmful;
  • before starting a new or intensive exercise programme, or with an injury.

The target formulas in MEGO (Mifflin–St Jeor, activity factors, goal adjustments) are for healthy adults and do not account for medical conditions, pregnancy or medication.

3. Adults only

MEGO is for adults aged 18 and over (pp_7). Registration requires the "I am 18 or older" confirmation; the app does not verify age by other means. The formulas are not designed for children or adolescents.

4. Targets, weight loss and fasting

4.1 Suggested calorie targets never go below the calculated BMR and are never applied without your confirmation. Targets you enter yourself are used as entered.

4.2 The app warns when weight is dropping "faster than 1% of body weight per week — risk of muscle loss". These warnings are based only on the data you log and are not a medical assessment.

4.3 The fasting timer only records times; it does not assess whether fasting is safe for you.

4.4 Reminders (water, meals, weigh-in) fire while the app is open or in the background; reliable alerts when the app is fully closed are not available. Do not rely on MEGO for medication or medical reminders.

5. Exercise, training and recovery

5.1 Exercise pages give general technique guidance with original illustrations: "General technique guidance, not medical advice. Stop if you feel pain and ask a qualified professional." (exd_disclaimer).

5.2 Recovery indicators "are not a medical assessment. Pain or unusual symptoms → stop and consult a professional." (rl_disclaimer).

5.3 The estimated one-repetition maximum is "a comparison tool between sessions, not a max to test". Calories burned are estimates that can differ from real expenditure by 20–30 %.

6. Allergies and dietary restrictions

Dietary preference tags (e.g. dairy, eggs, tree nuts, peanuts, soy, gluten, sesame, shellfish) are "used by the Coach and Food intelligence only". MEGO is not an allergen-safety tool: food records, Open Food Facts data, AI photo results and recipes may be incomplete or wrong about ingredients. Always check product labels if you have an allergy or intolerance.
