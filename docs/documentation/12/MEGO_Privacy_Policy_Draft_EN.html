2.2 Without an account (guest mode)

Your records stay in your browser's storage on that device only. They are not sent to MEGO's cloud. The services in section 7 that load the app (GitHub Pages, Google Fonts) and Open Food Facts lookups still apply.

2.3 What we do not collect

MEGO has no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools. Photos taken with the scanner are not stored by MEGO. MEGO does not ask for your location; photo location metadata is removed on your device before upload.

3. Health-related data

5.3 Some services listed in section 7 (Google, GitHub, Open Food Facts) process data outside Saudi Arabia. [Cross-border transfer wording — to be decided after legal review.]

6. Who can see your data

6.1 Only you can read and write your private records. Server-side security rules (Firestore rules) block every other account, including administrators. Your own access is allowed only while your account is active.

6.2 Administrators can see the account card only: name, e-mail, country, account status and dates (creation, last activity). They can change only the status (suspend / reactivate). They also see foods you submit for the shared database, error reports (no diary content) and AI usage figures (counts only).

6.3 Passwords are never visible to MEGO or administrators.

6.4 We do not sell or share your data with anyone for advertising or marketing.

8. Meal photos and AI

8.1 If you use the photo scanner, the photo is resized and re-encoded on your phone, which removes all metadata (EXIF: GPS location, device, time); only the pixels are sent.

8.2 It is sent to Google's Gemini API free tier. On the free tier Google may use this content to improve its products and human reviewers may see it, disconnected from your account. Do not photograph people or documents. Photos are not stored by MEGO (pp_4).

8.3 The scanner is refused for accounts whose country is in the EU, EEA, UK or Switzerland (free-tier terms).

8.4 The result is only a suggestion: nothing is saved until you review and confirm it.

9. Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Server-side rules allow each account to read and write only its own records, and only while the account is active; an older copy can never overwrite a newer one. Error reports contain the error message only, without diary content (pp_12). The AI key is kept only in the backend's Script Properties and is never sent to the browser. Details: MEGO_Security_Architecture, MEGO_User_Data_Protection.

10. Your rights and choices

  • View and correct your data in the app at any time.
  • Export all your data (CSV, XLSX, JSON) from Settings / Account.
  • Delete your account from the Account screen (password required) — this removes your cloud records, your profile, your login and this device's account copy.
  • Choose not to use the photo scanner or Open Food Facts lookups.
  • Use the app without an account (data on your device only).

For any other request, contact the operator (section 1). [Rights, response times and the competent authority depend on the applicable law — to be completed after legal review.]

11. Retention and deletion

Data is kept while your account exists; inactive accounts are not used for any other purpose (pp_6). Suspension does not delete data. Account deletion removes cloud records, profile and login immediately from the app. Error reports are kept until an administrator deletes them; scanner day counters can be removed after 8 days by an optional clean-up job. [Retention period for inactive accounts and for error reports; deletion of submitted foods after account deletion; Google's own retention on the Gemini free tier — to be decided / confirmed after legal review.] See MEGO_Data_Retention_and_Deletion.

12. Age

MEGO is for adults aged 18 and over. Registration requires an 18+ confirmation; the app does not otherwise verify age.
