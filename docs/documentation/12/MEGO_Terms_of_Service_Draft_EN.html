Purpose and scope

This document is the extended draft of the MEGO Terms of Service. It expands the in-app draft shown at #/terms (text keys tos_1–tos_11 in src/lib/i18n2.ts and src/lib/i18n3.ts) and the open items in docs/LEGAL-REVIEW.md. Every factual statement describes what the application actually does in the repository as of 7 November 2026. Text in [square brackets] is a placeholder that only the owner and legal counsel may complete. This draft is not legal advice and must not be published before legal review.

The terms apply to the MEGO web application (Progressive Web App) served at https://mego2012.github.io/Mego/, used either with a MEGO account or in "guest" mode without an account.

1. The service and the operator

1.1 MEGO is a nutrition-tracking application that lets you keep a food diary, set daily calorie and macronutrient targets, record body measurements, water, habits and fasting, plan meals, use recipes, view reports and read general nutrition education.

1.2 MEGO is operated by [Operator name, country and contact email — to be completed by the owner before launch.] ("the operator", "we").

1.3 MEGO is offered free of charge, "as is", without guarantees of availability (in-app text tos_1). There are no paid plans, no advertising and no in-app purchases in the current version.

2. Eligibility and registration

2.1 MEGO is intended for adults aged 18 and over. During registration you must tick "I am 18 or older"; the registration cannot be completed without it, and the server-side security rules refuse to create an account profile unless the age confirmation is recorded (ageConfirmed == true). The app does not verify your age by any other means.

2.2 To register you must also accept these Terms of Service and the Privacy Policy, provide a display name, an e-mail address, a password and your country.

2.3 Country and the AI scanner. If your account country is in the European Union, the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the free AI photo scanner is not offered to you, because it relies on the free tier of Google's Gemini API, whose terms do not allow this use for those regions. The rest of the service works normally. This restriction is enforced both in the app and in the backend.

3. Your account

3.1 Keep your password private. Passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication and are never visible to MEGO or to administrators.

3.2 You are responsible for the accuracy of the data you enter (foods, quantities, body measurements, targets).

3.3 You may change your display name and country in the app. You may request a password-reset e-mail from the sign-in screen.

3.4 One account belongs to one person. You must not share your account or use another person's account.

4. Use without an account (guest mode)

4.1 You may use MEGO without an account ("Continue without an account (this device only)"). In that case your records stay only in your browser on that device; they are not synchronised to MEGO's cloud. Loss of the browser data (for example clearing site data or losing the device) can permanently remove them unless you have exported a backup.

4.2 These terms apply to guest use as far as they are relevant.

5. Acceptable use

5.1 You must not abuse the service, attempt to access other accounts, or overload the AI scanner (tos_3). The detailed rules are in MEGO_Acceptable_Use_Policy, which forms part of these terms.

5.2 Accounts that break these terms may be suspended (see section 12).

7. AI photo scanner

7.1 The photo scanner gives estimates. Nothing is saved until you review and confirm it, and you remain responsible for what you log (tos_8).

7.2 When you use the scanner, the photo (resized on your device, with location and other metadata removed) is sent through the operator's Google Apps Script backend to the free tier of Google's Gemini API. On the free tier Google may use this content to improve its products and human reviewers may see it. Do not photograph people, documents or anything confidential. MEGO does not store the photos. Details are in MEGO_Privacy_Policy_Draft.

7.3 The number of scans is limited per account per day and globally per day (backend default: 10 scans per user per day; the operator can configure this). Google may also refuse requests when its free service is busy or its quota is used up.

7.4 The scanner is not available in the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland (section 2.3).

8. Health

8.1 MEGO provides general nutrition information and is not a medical device or a substitute for professional advice (pp_8).

8.2 Consult a qualified professional before making significant dietary changes, especially with a medical condition, pregnancy or an eating disorder (tos_5). See MEGO_Medical_Disclaimer.

9.2 You can view, correct, export (CSV, XLSX, JSON) and delete your data in the app at any time.

9.3 If you propose a food for the shared food database ("submit food"), you allow the operator's administrators to review it and, if approved, to publish it in the shared database visible to all signed-in users. [Licence wording for user-submitted foods — to be decided after legal review.]

11. Availability and free-tier limits

11.1 The service runs on free tiers (Firebase Spark plan, Apps Script quotas, Gemini API free tier). Free-tier limits of the underlying services apply; features may be temporarily unavailable when a limit is reached.

11.2 The app keeps a local copy of your data so most features work offline; changes are synchronised when the connection returns.

11.3 No service level, uptime or response-time commitment is given.

12. Suspension and ending your use

12.1 You can delete your account at any time from the Account screen. Deletion requires your password and removes your cloud records, your account profile, your login and this device's copy of your account data. It cannot be undone; export your data first if you want to keep it.

12.2 Administrators may suspend accounts that break these terms. A suspended account cannot read or write its cloud records (enforced by server-side rules) and cannot use the AI scanner. Suspension does not delete data: the app tells the user "Your data is kept and nothing has been deleted." An administrator can reactivate the account.

12.3 The in-app draft (tos_9) says "you can still export your data" after suspension. In the current version the suspended screen only offers sign-out and the cloud records cannot be read while suspended, so export during suspension is not available in the app. [The owner must decide how a suspended user can obtain a copy of their data, e.g. on request to the operator contact, and the wording must be corrected.]
