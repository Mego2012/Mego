## Who operates MEGO

[Operator name, country and contact email — to be completed by the owner before launch.] Requests about your data go to this contact.

## What we store

Your account email and display name, your country, and the records you create: food diary, your own foods and recipes, targets (calories, protein, fiber, vitamins and minerals), body measurements, workouts and cardio sessions, water, habits, fasting, meal plans, food preferences and settings. Progress photos stay on your device and are never uploaded.

## Health-related data

Weight, body measurements, food intake and goals can reveal information about your health. They are used only to show your own diary, reports and suggestions, never for advertising, and are not sold or shared.

## Where it is stored

In Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Authentication) under your account, and in your browser on your devices. Passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication and are never visible to MEGO.

## Where data is stored

Your synced records are stored in Google Cloud Firestore under your account. The database is located in the Google Cloud region me-central2 (Dammam, Saudi Arabia). Photos sent to the optional AI meal scanner are processed by Google’s Gemini API, which may process them outside Saudi Arabia, and are not stored by MEGO. Your device also keeps a local copy so the app works offline.

## Who can see it

Only you. Server-side security rules block every other account, including administrators, from your private records. Administrators can see your name, email, country, account status and last-activity date to manage accounts.

## Services that receive data

Google Firebase (accounts and your synced records); Google Apps Script and the Gemini API (only when you use the photo scanner); GitHub Pages (hosts the app and receives your IP address and browser details when you open it); Google Fonts (the typefaces are loaded from Google, which receives your IP address); Open Food Facts (only a scanned barcode number, or the words you type when you choose "search Open Food Facts"). MEGO has no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools.

## Meal photos and AI

If you use the photo scanner, the photo (resized, location data removed) and food names are sent to Google’s Gemini API free tier. On the free tier Google may use this content to improve its products and human reviewers may see it, disconnected from your account. Do not photograph people or documents. Photos are not stored by MEGO.

## Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Server-side rules allow each account to read and write only its own records. A suspended account can no longer add or change records, but can still export and delete its own data. Error reports contain the error message only, without diary content.

## Your rights

You can export all your data and delete your account at any time from Settings. Deleting the account removes your cloud data (including food proposals you submitted), your login and this device’s copy of the account.

## Your rights

You can view, correct, export and delete your data in the app at any time. For any other request, contact the operator above. [Rights, response times and the competent authority depend on the applicable law — to be completed after legal review.]

## Retention

Data is kept while your account exists. Inactive accounts are not used for any other purpose. Your browser also keeps the app's data for offline use, up to 13 recent automatic backup copies on this device (10 rolling + 3 taken before app updates), your language choice and a copy of the public service settings (no tracking cookies); deleting the account removes this device's copy.

## Age

MEGO is for adults aged 18 and over.

## Not medical advice

MEGO provides general nutrition information and is not a medical device or a substitute for professional advice.

## Changes to this policy

Material changes will be announced in the app before they take effect. The date of the current version is shown at the top of this page.
